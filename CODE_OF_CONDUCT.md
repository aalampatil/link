# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Link welcomes participants of every background and experience level. We are
committed to a community where people can contribute without harassment,
discrimination, or intimidation, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity,
gender identity or expression, nationality, race, religion, sexual orientation,
appearance, education, or socioeconomic status.

## Expected behavior

- Treat others with empathy, patience, and respect.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work and accept feedback in
  good faith.
- Respect differing experiences and viewpoints, and help newcomers participate.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and make a reasonable effort to repair harm.
- Respect privacy and keep confidential information out of public discussions.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, sexualized
content or unwanted sexual attention, and publishing someone's private
information without permission are unacceptable. Repeated disruption, spam,
and retaliation against someone who reports a concern are also unacceptable.

## Scope

These expectations apply to this repository's issues, pull requests, reviews,
and other project spaces, as well as public activity where someone officially
represents Link.

## Reporting concerns

Report incidents privately to the maintainer, Aalam Patil, at
[aalam.workacc@gmail.com](mailto:aalam.workacc@gmail.com), using the subject
`Link conduct report`. Include relevant links, dates, a description of the
incident, and any immediate concerns. You do not need to confront the person
involved or post a public report.

The maintainer will review reports fairly and handle information discreetly,
sharing it only as needed to investigate and respond. Retaliation against
reporters or people participating in an investigation is prohibited.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, reject contributions,
limit participation, or block accounts to protect the community. Decisions
consider the seriousness, impact, and repetition of the behavior. Responses
may include:

1. **Correction:** Explain the concern privately and request a change in behavior.
2. **Warning:** Give a formal warning and set clear participation conditions.
3. **Temporary restriction:** Suspend participation for a stated period after
   serious or repeated misconduct.
4. **Permanent exclusion:** End participation for severe abuse, threats,
   harassment, or a continuing pattern of harmful behavior.

Serious incidents may warrant an immediate restriction without earlier steps.
Where appropriate, the maintainer will explain the decision and its duration
privately. Requests to reconsider a decision may be sent to the same reporting
address with relevant context.
